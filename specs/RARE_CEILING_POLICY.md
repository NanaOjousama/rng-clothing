# Rare Ceiling Policy

The active rarity ladder is:

- **Common:** enhanced vanilla basics.
- **Uncommon:** higher-detail Fashion Sense clothing and broader normal garment variety.
- **Rare:** premium normal garments with minimal, elegant design.
- **Legendary:** Rare-quality construction plus a signature visual identity and stronger presentation.
- **Mythic:** the future absolute chase tier.

Rare may use excellent tailoring, high-detail rendering, premium collars, cuffs, sleeves, restrained layering, clean color blocking, and polished directional reconstruction. It must remain low on motifs, spectacle, dramatic asymmetry, and thematic storytelling.

A Rare garment may have one dominant construction idea and subordinate finishing. More than one major special hook triggers Legendary review. Hoodies begin at Legendary under the current policy.

Customization roles such as `PrimaryFabric`, `SecondaryFabric`, `Sleeve`, `Cuff`, `Collar`, `Lapel`, `Trim`, `Accent`, and a reserved `MotifSocket` remain semantic metadata. Exterior contour and structural seams are protected. This policy does not implement customization UI or currency.
