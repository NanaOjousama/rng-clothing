# Legendary Calibration Policy

Legendary is a premium garment with a signature identity. It inherits Rare-level construction quality, then adds one dominant identity system and one coordinated secondary region.

## Locked lower ceilings

- Uncommon upper edge: V6 Uncommon 21 (`Night Relay Zip Jacket`) and V6 Uncommon 22 (`Rosewake Sail Blouse`).
- Rare upper edge: `Juniper Vale Cardigan` and `Nocturne Silver Waistcoat`.

Legendary must clear those garments through silhouette, construction, thematic read, or coordinated layering—not through extra shading alone.

## Legendary floor

- One memorable silhouette or construction hook.
- One substantial supporting region that carries the identity into another direction or garment zone.
- Designed front, side, and back reads.
- A surface composition that remains recognizable without shading.
- Protected outline, structural seams, signature silhouette, and signature hook.

## Legendary ceiling

- At most two coordinated presentation systems in ordinary Legendary calibration.
- VFX, transformations, animated spectacle, and multi-stage motif systems remain reserved for future Mythic work.
- A Legendary may use coordinated Fashion Sense layers when the silhouette needs them; layers are not a substitute for design.
- A Legendary should remain wearable clothing rather than defaulting to a full costume.

## Renderer contract

- Hoodie: proven coordinated Shirt + native SleeveColors + Hat/Hood + preset contract.
- Other calibrated garments: Fashion Sense Shirt + native animated SleeveColors.
- No custom Sleeves atlas is emitted by this calibration.
- Signature overlays use existing palette roles, direct logical pixels, and article-role templates. They do not change anchors, body coverage, scale, or runtime assembly.

## Future customization

Fabric, collar, sleeve, cuff, trim, accent, pocket, strap, hood, and motif/emblem sockets may be exposed semantically. Exterior contour, structural seams, signature silhouette, and the signature hook remain protected.
