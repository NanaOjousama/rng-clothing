# Pixel-Art Clothing Grammar

## Pixel economy observed in successful references

Small human-made garments rarely outline every edge uniformly. They place dark pixels at high-information junctions and let adjacent value groups define the rest. Their strongest clusters explain construction rather than texture.

### High-value contour anchors

- collar recess and under-chin opening;
- outer shoulder turn;
- underarm/outer-layer overlap;
- lapel or cardigan edge termination;
- lower pocket corner;
- cuff/hand boundary;
- hem corner and inner-layer separation;
- hood-to-shoulder junction;
- side seam where volume changes.

These anchors should use the deepest structural dark. Long uninterrupted dark traces often make clothing harsh, flat, or smaller.

### Value grouping

- Keep each fabric as a coherent value family.
- Reserve the darkest value for recesses, overlap and selected contour anchors.
- Use a midtone for most fabric area; highlights should describe planes, not sprinkle texture.
- Maintain a readable value boundary between inner and outer layers even when hues are similar.
- Collar interiors, under-lapels, underarms, pocket openings and lower overhangs are reliable shadow zones.

### Light and form

Use an upper-left-biased light consistently but subordinate it to construction. Useful highlights are short shoulder ridges, lapel/collar top planes, a pocket-flap edge, an upper sleeve plane, or one knit ridge. Useful shadows are collar recess, right/lower side plane, cuff underside and hem overlap.

### Small-cluster semantics

- One logical pixel: seam break, piping, button, zipper pull, cuff edge, small fold.
- Two-to-four pixel cluster: pocket flap, bow/tie knot, buckle, patch, knit diamond, small original motif.
- Short line: placket, yoke edge, ribbed band, fold; its endpoints must explain where construction stops.
- Repeats: use a stable rhythm with quiet gaps. Noise is not fabric.

### Material cues

- **Knit:** soft value transitions, ribbed terminations, sparse coherent repeats.
- **Woven shirt:** crisp collar/placket, flatter planes, controlled checks/stripes.
- **Work shell:** firmer contour anchors, large pockets/panels, restrained highlights.
- **Formal fabric:** clean dark/light separation, sharp lapel planes, minimal texture.
- **Padded winter shell:** broad rounded mass and a few horizontal quilting divisions.
- **Sport shell:** clean high-contrast panels, zipper/stand collar, very little incidental texture.

## Using the project's extra resolution

Fashion Sense resolution should buy clearer collar depth, true open-layer edges, shaped hems, pocket topology, yokes, cuffs, wrap overlaps, controlled patterns and direction-specific reconstruction. It should not merely add more outline pixels or noise to an 8×8 idea.

Prioritize: coverage → article silhouette → collar/opening/layering → hem/sleeve relationship → major panels → surface design → finish. A higher-resolution candidate fails when a simpler reference communicates the article faster.

## Rejection conditions

Reject muddy ramps with no dominant midtone; uniform black tracing; isolated highlight speckles; seams that cross layer boundaries; patterns that ignore side/back continuation; details visible only enlarged; or shading used to fake an absent collar/opening/hem.
