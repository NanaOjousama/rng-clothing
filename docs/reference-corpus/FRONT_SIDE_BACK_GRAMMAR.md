# Front, Side, and Back Garment Grammar

## Direction is reconstruction, not rotation

The reviewed Fashion Sense shirts explicitly provide four views. Strong sources preserve the article's *topology* while reallocating pixels by direction. Front-only features compress or disappear at the side; rear carriers replace front openings at the back. Weak side views look like narrow crops of the front because they retain front stripes but lose depth, overlap, and hem logic.

The project-facing canonical review order is **Front | Right | Back | Left**. Source data often declares `Front`, `Right`, `Left`, `Back`; labels must be mapped semantically rather than assumed from atlas position.

## What each view must answer

### Front

- What is the neckline/collar mass?
- Is the garment closed, partially opened, fully opened, zipped, wrapped, or overlapped?
- Where do outer and inner layers separate?
- How does the hem terminate?
- Which single primary construction gesture dominates?

### Right and left

- How deep is the collar, hood, lapel, or shawl edge?
- Does an outer shell sit over an inner layer?
- How wide/soft/rigid is the torso mass?
- What happens to pockets, cuffs, belt/sash, hem and shoulder panels at the side seam?
- Is asymmetry genuinely directional? Left and right may differ when construction demands it.

### Back

- What replaces the front opening?
- Is the rear carrier a yoke, hood, collar band, knit field, tailored seam, sash, tabard panel, or quiet fabric mass?
- Does the rear hem match the article type?
- Is there one coherent secondary designed region without copying the front motif?

## Family-specific translation rules

| Family | Front | Side | Back |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tee | neck binding; optional front motif | sleeve edge and neck depth; motif usually gone | quiet neck binding; optional small upper-back echo |
| Button shirt | collar, placket, pocket | collar projection, cuff, side seam; pocket compresses | rear collar and yoke; no placket/buttons |
| Open shirt | two leaves around broad inner tee | one outer edge plus inner neck | unified outer fabric with yoke |
| Cardigan | soft knit edges and inner shirt | one hanging edge, cuff, pocket/hem cue | single knit mass and rear neck band |
| Sweater vest | neck/armhole binding over shirt | vest edge separates from inner sleeve | knit back with armhole bounds |
| Waistcoat | deep V, buttons, shaped points | tapered waist and shirt sleeve | tailored back/adjuster cue |
| Hoodie | hood opening and pouch/zip | hood projects behind head, joins shoulder | hood mass becomes primary feature |
| Track jacket | stand collar, zipper, sport panels | sleeve stripe and collar dominate | shoulder band wraps; zipper disappears |
| Bomber | rounded shell, zip, gathered band | rounded short shell and pocket angle | broad rounded yoke and ribbed band |
| Work jacket | collar, large patch pockets, straight hem | one pocket mass, cuff, heavy side seam | broad yoke, simple durable lower back |
| Field jacket | collar/guard, yoke, chest utility | shell edge, cuff, one utility panel | disciplined yoke and vertical seams |
| Blazer | lapels, shirt, button stance, pockets | lapel thickness, fitted waist, pocket edge | rear collar, shoulder seam, center seam/vent |
| Sailor top | broad collar and V | collar wraps over shoulder | rear square/tapered collar panel |
| Wrap top | overlapping diagonal panels and sash | overlap terminates; sash wraps | sash continuation and clean rear shoulder |
| Tunic | neck slit, belt, lower panel | belt wrap and side split | belt and rear upper seam |
| Tabard | broad over-panel over underlayer | over-panel compresses; underlayer remains | rear panel/attachment, reduced motif |

## Detail migration rules

- **Front-only:** buttons on a placket, chest motif, tie knot, paired chest pockets, zipper pull.
- **Compress at side:** lapel, inner-shirt field, one pocket edge, wrap overlap, pouch, tabard panel.
- **Wrap through side:** yoke, shoulder band, cuff, waist band, hem trim, sash, all-over knit/pattern.
- **Reconstruct at back:** rear collar, yoke, hood, adjuster, center seam, vent implication, tabard attachment.
- **Terminate intentionally:** chest stripe at side seam, diagonal harness line, front motif, lapel edge.

## Directional rejection rules

Reject when a side view retains a bright vertical front stripe but loses the opening it represented; when left is merely mirrored despite asymmetric closure; when a rear view repeats buttons or lapels; when a hood/collar vanishes behind the neck; when hem height or ribbing changes without construction reason; or when the side/back can no longer identify the article after palette removal.
