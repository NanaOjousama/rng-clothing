# V4 Art Grammar Pass 2 — Construction Grammar

This pass builds on Pass 1's ownership correction. It keeps the cleaned body planes, then makes component mappings more construction-aware at 16x16.

Implemented:
- shallow bounded polo collar clusters in all four directions;
- crew collar direction-specific clusters;
- sweater-vest V binding shortened to upper chest instead of an exaggerated deep V;
- side vest armhole binding shortened to the actual upper armhole region rather than a full side rail;
- straight hems changed from full dark bars to sparse termination cues;
- knit hems/waistbands/ribbing mapped back to PrimaryFabric so they read as knit construction instead of arbitrary contrast belts;
- sailor collar side/back mappings reduced to directional construction cues rather than long vertical stripes.
