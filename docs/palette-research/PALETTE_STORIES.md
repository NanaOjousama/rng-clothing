# Project-Owned Palette Stories

The exact original semantic selections are stored in [`palette_stories.json`](../../specs/palettes/palette_stories.json). They are not Lospec palette copies. Each story defines role colors, mood, temperature, shadow behavior, saturation hierarchy, compatible materials, unsuitable uses, and an anti-convergence note.

## Story library

### Everyday and light color

- **Everyday Neutral:** stone/taupe with a localized clay note.
- **Warm Everyday:** terracotta cloth, oat cream and restrained ochre.
- **Cool Everyday:** muted blue-teal with a small warm counterpoint.
- **Soft Charcoal:** low-saturation graphite with full value separation.
- **Cream Academic:** warm cream framed by plum and antique tan.
- **Coastal Mist:** mist aqua, sand cream and a small coral note.
- **Sea Glass:** weathered green glass with mineral light and coral support.
- **Spring Orchard:** light orchard green, peach cream and berry accent.
- **Lavender Day:** dusty lavender, warm cream and restrained rose.
- **Rosewood Pastel:** rosewood cloth, peach cream and caramel accent.

### Seasonal, natural and workwear

- **Autumn Knit:** rust knit with muted ochre and warm yellow focal.
- **Harvest Workwear:** golden canvas, field green and ember tab.
- **Forest Library:** forest cloth, aged parchment and book-cloth red.
- **Winter Frost:** frost blue, ice neutral and cool lavender.
- **Snowberry:** pale mauve-gray with dusty and vivid berry hierarchy.
- **Denim Blue:** indigo work cloth, canvas tan and copper-rust.
- **Earth & Moss:** moss, clay and localized amber.

### Sport, street and vivid

- **Bright Sport:** athletic blue, light neutral and compact orange.
- **Retro Track:** muted violet, mustard and localized coral.
- **Street Neon:** slate and pale neutral with neon mint or pink focal.
- **Cyber Night:** indigo/teal technical cloth with magenta and tiny cyan.
- **High-Contrast Statement:** light stone, teal structural block and one warm focal.

### Formal, dark and statement

- **Formal Pearl:** pearl body, cool slate framing and muted wine/metal support.
- **Formal Midnight:** midnight blue around a substantial warm-ivory layer.
- **Luxury Neutral:** mushroom/linen tailoring with muted mauve or brass.
- **Gothic Silver:** graphite, pale silver and a controlled plum focal.
- **Sailor Navy:** ocean blue, sailcloth cream and one red note.
- **Celestial Dusk:** dusk violet/blue with one warm star focal.
- **Sun Ember:** ember orange, indigo support and small sun-yellow accent.
- **Ceremonial Ivory:** ivory, plum framing and controlled red/antique-metal support.

## Usage rules

1. A story is a role relationship, not a mandatory use of every listed color.
2. Material behavior may reduce highlight/secondary/motif roles.
3. Palette story does not imply gender, garment family, theme or rarity.
4. Reusing a story on two structurally distinct garments is permitted, but does not itself create diversity.
5. The same grammar recolored with multiple stories remains one structural design.
6. Batch planning must balance dominant values, temperature and saturation, not merely story IDs.

## Coverage balance

The 30 stories deliberately include:

- light-, middle-, and dark-primary compositions;
- warm, cool, neutral and mixed-temperature relationships;
- pastels suitable for masculine/unisex clothing;
- workwear without making earth tones the default;
- restrained and vivid sport systems;
- neon with controlled coverage;
- formal light and formal dark options;
- statement palettes that do not depend on black/gold/purple.

No story is reserved for Common, Uncommon, Rare, Legendary, or Mythic.
