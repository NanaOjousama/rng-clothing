# V6 Rare Ceiling Calibration

## Result

The six-garment batch establishes a credible Rare ceiling:

> Rare is an expensive, premium normal garment. Legendary adds a signature identity and stronger presentation.

All six use direct 16x16 Fashion Sense shirt art at Scale 2 and the approved post-V5E edge/shade polish. They preserve the safe native Farmer sleeve path and do not export the rejected custom Sleeves atlas. No garment uses a hood, Hat, Accessory, VFX, reactive state, dramatic asymmetry, emblem system, or signature narrative hook.

The batch contains a premium crewneck, fitted fine-knit henley, refined cardigan, polished waistcoat, minimal jacket, and premium work shirt. Their article identities and dominant construction gestures remain distinct.

## Ceiling calibration

- Premium shaping, collar depth, cuffs, ribbing, lapels, pockets, layered fronts, fabric hierarchy, and reconstructed side/back views are valid Rare spending.
- Each garment has one primary construction idea and one subordinate finishing system.
- Visible motifs are absent. `MotifSocket` is preserved as empty semantic customization metadata.
- The normal 32-pixel gameplay footprint is maintained. Premium quality comes from pixel placement and hierarchy, not physical enlargement.
- Adding a prominent crest, dramatic asymmetry, weather state, animation, VFX, or a second major focal hook would trigger Legendary review.

## Gameplay-size judgment

**Nocturne Silver Waistcoat** is the strongest calibration piece. Its vest frame, inner-shirt relationship, button rhythm, and quiet back make it immediately premium without becoming theatrical.

**Juniper Vale Cardigan** is the strongest relaxed garment. The open edge and low pockets create a clear article identity; its bright inner layer is fashionable but still conventional clothing.

**Cedarline Reserve Jacket** is the cleanest expression of the upper Rare ceiling. It uses polished lapels, a restrained inner layer, one closure cue, and tailored corners. An emblem or more dramatic shoulder treatment would push it toward Legendary, so neither is present.

**Harborline Merino Crew** is the most decorated item. Its coordinated knit band remains a familiar textile treatment rather than a signature motif, so it stays within Rare. It is the garment closest to the ceiling, but it does not cross it.

**Sea Glass Fine-Knit Henley** was initially too dark and flat. Its revised sea-glass fabric hierarchy improves the expensive fine-knit read without adding decoration. It remains the quietest piece and depends most on dye/palette quality.

**Orchard Reserve Workshirt** communicates premium workwear through its yoke, paired pockets, placket, and cuff treatment. It is intentionally practical rather than spectacular.
