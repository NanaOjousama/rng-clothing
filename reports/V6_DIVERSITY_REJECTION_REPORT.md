# V6 Diversity and Rejection Report

The final batch contains 12 Common, 12 Uncommon, and 6 Rare+ garments. The maximum within-tier eight-axis similarity is **0.750**. Common permits a slightly higher similarity ceiling because its locked brief intentionally excludes garment-category creep and decorative systems; it still varies fit, neckline, panel split, hem treatment, palette, and primary construction.

## Gate boundaries

All 30 pass binary-alpha, value-contrast, palette-restraint, and front/side/back reconstruction checks. These are guardrails, not proof of beauty. No batch garment is promoted to production or runtime pass automatically.
