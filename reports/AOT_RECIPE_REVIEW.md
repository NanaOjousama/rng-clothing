## Candidate 1 — Rampart Field Jacket

- strength/branch: STRONG / militaryCore;
- archetype/rarity: JACKET / Rare;
- construction: regular, open neck, wide collar, center-open, visible inner layer, structured hem, split outer panels;
- substantial features: contrast inner layer, broad collar;
- color blocking: tan outer panels over dark olive inner layer;
- deterministic side: `lapel_led`.

Review:

1. **Family coherence:** strongest direct match to the repeated cropped tan jacket language.
2. **Distinctness:** collar-led and tan/dark compared with the olive/warm alternate jacket.
3. **Construction rather than color:** split panels, open layer, broad collar, and firm hem all carry identity before palette.
4. **Copy risk:** low. No logo, insignia, exact pocket map, harness, or character-specific motif.
5. **Scale survival:** high for collar, opening, panel split, and waist. Unsupported pocket/harness cues cannot survive because no frozen semantic region owns them.
6. **Branch coverage:** core military jacket.

Decision: **SELECTED**. This is the mandatory close visual adaptation candidate. It is as close as the frozen vocabulary permits without painting exceptions.

## Set-level coherence answers

1. **One family?** Yes semantically: all five prioritize structured collars, fitted/regular practical mass, disciplined panels, and waist termination.
2. **Distinct garments?** Yes. The revised final set uses open split-panel Jacket, fitted yoke Button Shirt, and solid heavy Collar Sweater roles.
3. **Construction-led?** Yes. Palette is supportive; selected recipes use split outer layers, a fitted yoke/closure system, and an uninterrupted dark civilian mass with a broad collar and structured waist.
4. **Cosplay risk?** Low. No exact emblem, logo, pocket map, belt web, or named character outfit is encoded.
5. **Gameplay survival?** The large regions should survive. Pocket flaps, epaulets, and harness systems are not falsely represented by noise.
6. **Core plus civilian?** Yes. Rampart is the core field jacket, Cadence is the utility shirt/vest branch, and Nightwatch is the dark civilian/formal branch.

## Selection risk

The corrected success condition remains stricter than semantic validity. Rampart may still read as “tan field jacket” rather than immediately franchise-specific because the frozen renderer lacks explicit cropped-length, pocket-flap, shoulder-panel, and harness-line regions. The revised role-separated pool avoids compounding that ambiguity with a second near-identical jacket.
