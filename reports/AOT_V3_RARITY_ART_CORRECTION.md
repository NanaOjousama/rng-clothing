# AoT V3 Rarity Art-Direction Correction

Status: **PASS — static art review and profile-specific validation**. Runtime wardrobe review remains the final aesthetic boundary.

## Correction applied

The AoT profile no longer treats recognizable insignia-like geometry as forbidden. Exact faction logos and copied pixels remain excluded, but original compact regiment-style badges are allowed. Recognition now comes from the compound of construction, utility geometry, palette, and—where rarity permits—an original crest focal point.

No renderer, anchor, mask, resolution, V3 global rarity budget, or Fashion Sense runtime field changed. The correction only changes the `AoTInspired` profile vocabulary, its six profile recipes, and its profile-specific validation.

## Rarity art profiles

Each tier now has both a floor and ceiling:

| Tier | Systems | Front richness range | Color ceiling | Motif ceiling | Intended read |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Common | 1 | 5–16 cells | 2 | normally absent | nice clothing from this world |
| Uncommon | 2 | 10–32 cells | 3 | 8 front cells | definitely in the AoT-inspired pool |
| Rare | 3 | 28–42 cells | 3 | 9 front cells | premium AoT chase piece below future Legendary/Mythic |

Rare additionally requires 20–40 designed back cells, at least 8 designed cells on each side, a visible focal feature, a substantial secondary region, and finishing treatment. Motif and pattern may not both occupy the Rare front. This prevents “maximize every system” behavior.

## Hero-jacket result

Rookfield now combines:

- cropped tan split-shell silhouette;
- wide structured lapel/collar;
- fitted olive inner layer;
- shoulder-block/epaulette suggestion;
- dark outer-panel and lapel anchors;
- short structured waist;
- asymmetric cream/gold split-wing crest on the chest;
- one/two-cell crest continuation on the right side;
- deliberate back yoke and collar treatment;
- natural side sleeve/body transition.

The crest is nine front cells and two right-side cells. It is visible at gameplay size but not garment-dominant. It does not reproduce the supplied insignia and leaves the back free of a large emblem. That restraint preserves future Legendary space.

Pocket/flap language remains simplified into upper-panel and dark utility-anchor geometry rather than adding a fourth surface system. A test with crest + explicit pocket repeat + lapel trim required dropping the shoulder block and weakened the iconic upper-shell read, so the controlled crest/shoulder/lapel composition was retained.

## Benchmark comparison

The corrected set was compared at exact gameplay footprint against representative successful V3 garments:

- Common versus Inkline Dress Shirt: Hearthward uses the same calm one-gesture design scale; Mossline is more directionally designed without exceeding the Common ceiling.
- Uncommon versus Brassfield Short Jacket: Palisade and Crosswall now have large structural regions; Crosswall’s small badge supplies a clear focal cue without approaching Rare density.
- Rare versus Emberbolt Layer Jacket: Rookfield reaches comparable authoredness through its crest, shoulder shell, lapels, inner layer, and back/side continuation. Nightward remains deliberately quieter and formal rather than imitating Emberbolt’s large asymmetric motif.

Rookfield’s 39 front cells sit below the profile ceiling of 42 and below the global V3 ceiling of 46. Its three surface colors also remain below the global four-color allowance. This is premium but not maximal.

## Future tier space preserved

Reserved for Legendary:

- larger or coordinated front/back emblems;
- richer back motifs;
- additional Fashion Sense layers;
- unusual asymmetric construction;
- more ambitious silhouettes.

Reserved for Mythic:

- bespoke chase-piece composition;
- maximum thematic recognition;
- curated special layering;
- subtle animation/VFX where technically appropriate;
- intentional exceptions to normal garment grammar.

Rare does not use animation, VFX, extra layers, a dominant back emblem, four simultaneous surface systems, or maximum-density multi-view decoration.

## Critical visual judgment

- **Strongest improvement:** Rookfield. The cream split-wing cluster creates the missing immediate premium recognition cue while the jacket remains clearly readable.
- **Best rarity step:** Crosswall versus Mossline. Both are olive field garments, but Crosswall now has structured side panels plus a controlled badge and reads unmistakably more authored.
- **Most restrained Rare:** Nightward. It passes the richness floor through large side-panel composition, formal closure, waist cadence, and finishing—not through an emblem. It remains less immediately iconic than Rookfield, which is intentional but should be checked in-game.
- **Remaining weakest garment:** Hearthward. It is a valid low-ceiling Common, but still the least exciting roll.

The corrected rarity progression now reads as calm world clothing → explicit themed utility clothing → premium recognizable hero garment, without treating Rare as the generator’s final possible form.
