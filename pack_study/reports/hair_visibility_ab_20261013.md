# Hair Visibility A/B QC — 2026-10-13

## Purpose
Compare Fashion Sense `HideHair` true/false for three QC2 hats without altering any image pixels, origin positions, frame sizes, side flip settings, or the installed QC2 pack.

- Source installed pack: `[FS] PackStudy QC v2` (7 hats), unchanged.
- New isolated installed pack: `[FS] PackStudy Hair Policy QC` (`PackStudy.HairPolicyQC`, six hats).
- Pairs: QC2 01 propeller cap, QC2 05 messy straw, QC2 10 blue straw; only Name and per-view `HideHair` differ.
- Source sprite bytes for each ON/OFF pair are identical to the source QC2 `hat.png` and all remaining hat JSON properties match exactly (programmatic verification passed).

## Findings (visual preliminary; awaiting user approval)
- **Hat 01 propeller cap:** HAIR ON retains believable hair around forehead, side, back; HAIR OFF removes virtually all visible hair and leaves a shaved/bare look. Existing visor slope is original authored art and not fixed by either policy.
- **Hat 05 messy straw:** HAIR OFF creates empty-looking forehead/back-of-neck; HAIR ON generally looks preferable for styles 0 and 38. For hairstyle 16, HAIR ON shows brown hair pixels *above* the top of the straw crown (front/back), visibly clipping. Original authored broad brim side shape is independent of hair policy.
- **Hat 10 blue straw:** Same tradeoff as Hat 05. HAIR ON looks better overall for 0/38, but hairstyle 16 pokes above the crown at front/left. HAIR OFF avoids crown penetration by completely hiding hair, with unacceptable bare-looking sides and neck.

## Policy recommendation (not yet implemented)
Preserve inherited author's `HideHair` state by default; do not globally hide hair for open brimmed hats. For hats with verified full-head coverage, use the author's `HideHair` policy or require visual proof for a different choice. Flag known protruding hairstyle/hat pairs for targeted layer/coverage or fit correction. Keep geometry/silhouette issues separate from hair settings. Seek user review before promoting this to generator rules.
